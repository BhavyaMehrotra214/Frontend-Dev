Welcome to the City of Light

Paris, the capital of France, is known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its picturesque cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

Famous Landmarks

From the iconic Eiffel Tower to the Louvre Museum and Notre-Dame Cathedral, Paris boasts world-famous attractions that draw millions of visitors each year.

Rich Cultural Heritage

Paris is home to over 130 museums, countless theaters, and historical monuments. The city thrives on creativity, from classical opera to modern art galleries.

Culinary Delights

Parisian cuisine offers everything from fresh croissants and baguettes to gourmet dining experiences at Michelin-starred restaurants.
